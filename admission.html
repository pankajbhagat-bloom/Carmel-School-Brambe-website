Documents required for registration

  • 2 passport size photographs of the child
  • Copy of Birth Certificate
  • Copy of Aadhar Card
  • Copy of Caste Certificate of the child or parent

Also required if coming from another school (LKG to Class 6)

  • PEN Number (UDISE+)
  • Original Transfer Certificate (TC) of the previous school
  • Original Conduct / Character Certificate
  • Original Marksheet of the final exam
Please note: keep the slip of the Registration Form safe — it must be produced on the day of interview / admission. Registration does not guarantee admission. All documents must be submitted at the time of admission or within one month of admission.
