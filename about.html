Our Name & Heritage

Carmel means an orchard or garden. As a proper name, "Mount Carmel" designates a well-known mountain range overlooking the Mediterranean Sea in Israel. Due to its panoramic vista and luxuriant vegetation, the prophets of the Bible saw an image of God's beauty in it — a sign of God's love and blessings for His people.

⛪

Our Roots

It is on this Mount that Prophet Elijah established the worship of one true God and served the oppressed. The Carmelite Order, a Religious Congregation in the Catholic Church, had its origin here. Its members dedicate themselves to the service of humanity without distinction of caste, creed or race. Carmel School is an expression of these values.

🌟

Our Mission

Carmel School commits itself to co-operate with local people to evolve enlightened youth — men and women of character — who would be of best service to humanity. Although open to boys and girls of all communities, it is a minority community school run by Carmel Mission, enjoying all rights and privileges enshrined in Articles 29 and 30 of the Indian Constitution.

Our Philosophy

Since an all-round development of the child is envisaged, the school discipline is geared to inculcate values like honesty, generosity, self-discipline, spirit of service and sense of fraternity. These values are to be the student's constant companions — always and everywhere — and his great accomplishment when he bids farewell to the Alma Mater.
