Who We Are

The Spirit of
Mount Carmel

🏔️

Our Name & Heritage

Carmel means an orchard or garden. As a proper name, "Mount Carmel" designates a well-known mountain range overlooking the Mediterranean Sea in Israel. Due to its panoramic vista and luxuriant vegetation, the prophets of the Bible saw an image of God's beauty in it — a sign of God's love and blessings for His people.

⛪

Our Roots

It is on this Mount that Prophet Elijah established the worship of one true God and served the oppressed. The Carmelite Order, a Religious Congregation in the Catholic Church, had its origin here. Its members dedicate themselves to the service of humanity without distinction of caste, creed or race. Carmel School is an expression of these values.

🌟

Our Mission

Carmel School commits itself to co-operate with local people to evolve enlightened youth — men and women of character — who would be of best service to humanity. Although open to boys and girls of all communities, it is a minority community school run by Carmel Mission, enjoying all rights and privileges enshrined in Articles 29 and 30 of the Indian Constitution.

Our Philosophy

Since an all-round development of the child is envisaged, the school discipline is geared to inculcate values like honesty, generosity, self-discipline, spirit of service and sense of fraternity. These values are to be the student's constant companions — always and everywhere — and his great accomplishment when he bids farewell to the Alma Mater.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Carmel School Brambe among the top schools in Ranchi?

Carmel School Brambe is an ICSE-affiliated school located in Brambe, near the Central University of Jharkhand (CUJ) campus on the outskirts of Ranchi. With a values-based curriculum rooted in the Carmelite tradition, experienced faculty and a 100% ICSE board pass rate, it is regarded as one of the well-established schools serving the Ranchi and Mandar region.

Which board is Carmel School Brambe affiliated with?

The school is affiliated with the ICSE board (Council for the Indian School Certificate Examinations) and offers classes from Nursery through Class 11.

Where exactly is Carmel School Brambe located?

The school is located in Brambe, Mandar, Ranchi, Jharkhand — 835205 — close to the CUJ (Central University of Jharkhand) campus.

What classes does Carmel School Brambe offer?

Carmel School Brambe offers Pre-Primary (Nursery–KG), Primary (Classes 1–5), Middle School (Classes 6–8), Secondary School (Classes 9–10), and the newly launched Senior Secondary (Class 11).

How do I apply for admission to Carmel School Brambe?

Admissions are open each academic year from Nursery to Class 11. You can reach out through the enquiry form on this website or contact the school office directly using the details in the Contact section below.
